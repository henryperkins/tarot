## Continuation gates

Accepting the bounded React bridge is separate from full-deck completion. Preserve the common occurrence, source-target, renderer and focus contract when adding entries; do not create a separate focus system per card. Every promoted card needs meaningful supported imagery and an intentional static, finite or living treatment, verified upright/reversed and compact, with understandable reduced-motion illumination, bounded lifecycle, later return and relationship support. Static local illumination is a valid authored choice. Whole-card fallback does not fill missing detail coverage. Other editions retain whole-card behavior without borrowing this edition's geometry.

Production passage metadata remains required continuation work under a subsequent authorized plan. Generation-time metadata or a validated alignment pass must identify run/spread occurrences, supported edition detail IDs, exact raw UTF-16 passage ranges and quotes, later returns and independently validated personal-context references. Validate the delivered raw prefix and each fully available range. Source replacement, mismatched identity/edition and unsupported structure fail closed. Card-name/keyword matching alone cannot establish an authored interpretation or psychological fact. The three recorded sidecars are fixture evidence, not a generalized service.

Optimize delivery before promotion: load only the user's spread and needed focus assets; bound overlays to visible or explicitly held targets. The manifest records 276,071,643 bytes for the complete study collection: do not transfer that collection into the product. Measure decode, memory and long-reading behavior on a physical mid-range handset. Emulated responsive checks do not prove physical-device frame pacing or GPU compositing.

Further gates include guest and real authenticated Pro coverage, affected subscription tiers, upright/reversed rendered geometry, compact and text zoom framing, reduced-motion and hidden/offscreen lifecycle, larger recorded spreads/shared relationships, and optimized asset delivery. Deterministic roster tests and three fixture readings cannot prove every interpretation or presentation. No production deployment or full-feature completion is asserted here.
