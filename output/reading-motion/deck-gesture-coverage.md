# Deck gesture coverage

The adopted study edition is `rws-immanuelle-vector`. All **78 canonical cards now have authored vector detail treatments**, totaling **151 supported detail IDs**. This inventory excludes the card back and maps every card to the checked-in vector manifest. The shared renderer retains whole-card identity, orientation, source occurrence identity, and fallback when a passage does not establish a supported detail.

The expansion adds **140 static detail treatments across the previously uncovered 70 cards**. They use soft elliptical illumination and authored focal crops. The Star remains the only card with masked living-water recipes (two pours). The earlier Ace of Wands, Seven of Swords, and Wheel of Fortune retain three finite traces; the remaining 146 details are intentionally static. Arrival, focus, and selection transitions can accompany static details. This is full-deck authored detail coverage, not 78 cinemagraphs or certification of every rendered state.

Authors inspected the actual upright SVGs and reviewed the new soft-region placements in rendered contact sheets. Those reviews establish first-pass upright image alignment; they do not establish exact silhouette segmentation, every phone crop, reversed pixel alignment, or physical-device performance. Authored literal probe sentences accompany each new detail and are labeled as test copy, never recorded personalized readings. Integration test outcomes belong in the [React bridge verification record](react-bridge-verification.md), separately from this geometry inventory.

The earlier bounded Chromium fixture evidence remains below: all three React examples exercised 6 / 6 / 25 associations. Repeated returns and requested detail targets were checked structurally. Upright Star desktop/phone and upright Ace/Queen phone pairing were visually inspected. Compact reduced-motion five-card rendering passed, without independent visual certification of every detail. Standalone evidence remains distinct from React evidence. All-78 identity/orientation unit coverage is structural only.

Each asset link uses this study edition. Authored mask/frame geometry belongs to the upright artwork plane; reversal is applied once to the common image/overlay plane. Existing RWS scan touch points do not validate vector geometry. Original SVG presence and static XML validation are asset evidence only. [Original hashes and provenance](assets/rws-immanuelle/manifest.json) and [artwork attribution and acquisition notes](assets/rws-immanuelle/README.md) remain unchanged. The source credits Pamela Colman Smith's artwork and Immanuelle's vectorization.
