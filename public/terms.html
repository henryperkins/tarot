2. What a reading is, and isn’t

Tableu offers tarot readings for reflection, self-exploration and entertainment. Readings are written by AI models from the cards drawn, their positions and what you share. They can be inaccurate or incomplete, and the same cards can produce different readings.

  • Readings don’t predict a fixed future. They describe possibilities and likely paths if things stay as they are. Your choices remain yours.
  • Readings aren’t professional advice. They don’t replace medical, mental health, legal, financial or other professional help. Talk to a qualified professional before making decisions in those areas.
  • In a crisis, get help now. If you’re thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or you’re in danger, contact your local emergency services. In the US, you can call or text 988.
