Using Tableu in ChatGPT

How do I connect Tableu?
Add the Tableu app in ChatGPT. When asked, sign in to your Tableu account and choose Allow.
How do I get a reading?
Ask in your own words, for example “Draw a three-card reading about my week.” Tableu draws the cards, then writes the reading. Open questions such as “What should I understand about…?” work better than yes-or-no questions.
How do I save a reading?
Say “save this.” Tableu saves the reading to your journal and confirms the entry. Saving the same reading twice doesn’t create a duplicate.
Can I add my own reflections?
Yes. Tell ChatGPT what you noticed about a card or the whole reading, and ask to add it to the journal entry.
How do I disconnect?
Remove Tableu in your ChatGPT settings. Your journal stays in your Tableu account.

Account and sign-in

I forgot my password.
Choose Forgot password when you sign in. We’ll email you a reset link, which expires after 30 minutes.
I didn’t get a verification or reset email.
Check your spam folder, then request a new email. Verification links expire after 24 hours. If nothing arrives, email us from the address on your account.
How do I change my profile or reading preferences?
Open your Account page and edit Profile or Reading Preferences.

Plans and billing

How do I change or cancel my plan?
On your Account page, under Subscription, choose Manage subscription. Payments are handled securely by Stripe.
I paid, but my plan hasn’t changed.
Use Restore purchases on your Account page. If that doesn’t fix it, email us with the email address you used at checkout.
Can I get a refund?
Our Terms of Service cover refunds. If you think you were charged in error, email us.

Your data

How do I export my journal?
On your Account page, under Privacy & Data, choose Export PDF or Export CSV. To get a copy of your account data, choose Download account data.
How do I delete my account?
On your Account page, under Privacy & Data, choose Delete account. This cancels any active subscription and can’t be undone, so export anything you want to keep first.
What does Tableu remember about me?
Your Account page lists the memories Tableu uses to personalize readings. You can delete them one by one or clear them all.
How is my information used?
Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect, who we share it with and how long we keep it.

About readings

Is a reading professional advice?
No. Readings are for reflection. They don’t replace a doctor, therapist, lawyer or financial adviser. If you’re in crisis, contact your local emergency services. In the US, you can call or text 988.
A reading or a shared note seems wrong or harmful.
Use Report on the note, or email us the share link or the time of the reading so that we can look into it.
Found a security issue?
Please email hello@lakefrontdigital.io with the details, and don’t share them publicly until we’ve had a chance to fix it.
