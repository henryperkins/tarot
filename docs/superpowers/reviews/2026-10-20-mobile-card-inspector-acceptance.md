## Evidence corrections and limits

- The first resumed responsive run was invalidated by a Vite restart while a concurrent unit test created/removed its temporary `.env.local` canary. The trace records the lost dev-server connection and page reload. Final frontend browser verification runs without that concurrent mutation.
- Scroll restoration is compared with the body position actually captured at opening. Browser automation can move the reference slightly while making it actionable; its pre-click position is recorded separately. The assertion still requires restored body styles, original-opener focus, a position within eight pixels, and subsequent document scrolling.
- Non-RWS failure fixtures block the seeded second scan before dealing can populate the decoded image cache. Registering a later route did not establish a failed image request and was corrected.
- The outside-artwork second-finger sequence was reproduced both in a built-app probe and as a failing browser assertion before the capture cancellation repair; the final gesture checks reject it in both engines.
- Waiting for the disclosure animation and requiring the last explanation to be visible after rotation reproduced the Symbols height defect in both engines with normal motion (reduced-motion cases passed). The animation library preserved a numeric final height despite manual cleanup. Ending at `auto` fixes reflow; the stronger checks passed in both engines with motion on/off, and their settled screenshots replace the earlier incomplete captures.
- Early sample transfer totals of approximately 2.98MB versus 0.57MB used different random spreads. They are not a controlled same-spread performance benchmark. The final cold-journey source/transfer records establish the selected resources for each measured run.
- Chromium/WebKit browser automation and CDP input are emulation evidence. Physical iOS Safari/Android Chrome browser chrome, native Back/edge gestures, pinch zoom, real safe-area behavior, VoiceOver and TalkBack still require device/assistive-technology acceptance. Pro and guest coverage does not qualify other subscription states.
