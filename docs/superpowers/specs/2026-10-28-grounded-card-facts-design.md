## Intent and approaches

Correcting the projection alone is the smallest alternative, but still permits unrestricted rewriting of literal descriptions. ## Contract

Keep a bounded, deck-scoped inventory of two verified relations: RWS Hierophant crossed keys at the figure's feet (existing annotation plus Waite and the original scan), and the traditional Thoth Art raven-on-skull motif on the cauldron (Crowley's text, without adding rim/inside precision). Expose no other stored location as newly verified canon. Existing imagery and symbolic meanings for other cards retain their present behavior.

For a reading containing one of these supported facts, the model returns a small JSON transport envelope with ordered prose blocks and optional fact-reference blocks. A fact block selects an approved fact ID and a supported caption language; it cannot supply replacement literal text. Prose blocks contain the normal reading, symbolic interpretation and optional practical guidance. The backend validates the envelope and every fact against the actual drawn cards, deck and final prompt inventory, then renders the approved localized caption into the existing reading string. Raw transport is archived for qualification and never becomes the consumer reading. Unknown, undrawn, cross-deck, missing-language, malformed or embellished references fail closed before rendering. A supported reference does not establish the factuality of other free prose; complete content review remains mandatory.

Preserve the exact reader question, roles, people, dates, negation, exclusions, names and orientations. Project the reviewed relation independently of whether a role has a prose template. The complete approved fact inventory and output contract are protected during fitting alongside the existing exact question/name contract; a prompt that cannot retain them fails closed at the existing cap. No retrieval score, threshold, gate, cap, passage label or provider changes.

The existing no-forecast criterion is binding. Retain the supplied Future label and its card meanings as a prospective symbolic lens. Do not infer a personal current course or its likely outcome from that position. This is an implementation choice to align source instructions with the unchanged criterion, not a new waiver or a retroactive reclassification. Supplied future events and literal dates stay intact.
